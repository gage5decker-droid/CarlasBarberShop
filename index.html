About Carla's

A True Neighborhood Barbershop

Walk in and you'll immediately know you're somewhere special. There's a barber pole out front, the TV might be playing The Andy Griffith Show, and Carla — with over 40 years behind the chair — will greet you like she's been expecting you.

This isn't a chain. There are no apps to download or strangers touching your hair. It's just Carla, her craft, and a genuine love for the people she serves. Customers who came in once have come back for years. Some call her a dear friend.

She's patient, warm, funny, and precise. She adapts to your style and actually listens. And when you leave, your hair will look great and your mood will be better than when you walked in.
